How it works

Zero pressure, maximum curiosity
i.

Pick a book

Each month the club settles on one AI/ML or data engineering book. Our sponsors often provide free e-books.

ii.

Read & discuss

We talk it over chapter by chapter on Discord — asynchronously, on your own schedule, from anywhere.

iii.

Meet the author

When we finish, we host a live conversation with the author. Ask them anything — literally.
